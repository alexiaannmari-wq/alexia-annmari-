Starting a conversation about operational support can feel like a big step, especially if you are not sure what you need yet. An intro call is usually a practical beginning rather than a pitch you have to prepare for perfectly.

We may talk about how your business currently runs, where time and attention are getting pulled, and what kind of ongoing help would make the biggest difference. You can share at your own pace. You are also welcome to ask about packages, communication rhythm, tools, and how ownership of work is handled.

By the end of the call, you should have a clearer sense of whether ongoing virtual administration feels like a useful next step.
