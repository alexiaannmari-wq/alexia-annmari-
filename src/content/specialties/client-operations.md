Client-facing operations should feel seamless — from first inquiry through active work and wrap-up — without you personally managing every step.

This area covers coordinating onboarding, preparing agendas and materials, organizing notes, tracking proposals and signatures, and keeping milestones visible so renewals, handoffs, and next steps do not stall.
