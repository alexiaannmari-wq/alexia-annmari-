Inbox and calendar work is often where time disappears — messages waiting for replies, meetings bouncing around, and details that only live in your head.

Support here includes organizing incoming communication, drafting or sending non-sensitive replies when appropriate, coordinating meetings, handling reschedules, and keeping reminders accurate so you can show up prepared without chasing every thread yourself.
